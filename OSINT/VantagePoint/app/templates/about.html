About VantagePoint

A modern, lightweight OSINT tool for intelligence analysts.

Vision

VantagePoint is designed to provide security analysts with a single, consolidated dashboard for all their OSINT needs. From IoC investigation to global threat intelligence feeds, VantagePoint brings everything into one unified, modern workspace.

Key Features

Unified Search

Query multiple OSINT APIs (VirusTotal, AlienVault, etc.) from a single search bar. No more tab hopping.

Real-time Feeds

Aggregated intelligence from the most reliable sources in the industry, kept up to date automatically.

Modular Architecture

Easily add or remove OSINT modules through the built-in management system. Every tool is customizable.

Lightweight & Secure

Built on Flask for performance and security, VantagePoint runs locally on your machine, ensuring your research remains private.
